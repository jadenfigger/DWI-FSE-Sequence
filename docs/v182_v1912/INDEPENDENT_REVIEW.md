## Verdict and findings

**No high- or medium-severity defect was established in the reviewed bytes.** The source is suitable for the documented, staged phantom/console test plan. This does not establish vendor compiler acceptance or physical RF/gradient correctness.

1. **LOW — little modeled compiler-image headroom remains.** v1.82 declarations/guards at lines 389–390 and 566–578, and v1.912 declarations/setup at lines 397–400 and 2372–2403, contribute to final modeled sizes of **56,590/56,664 bytes** and **52,542/52,568 bytes** respectively. Remaining margins are only **74 and 26 bytes**. The independent static audit passes all eight checks, with 25/18 forward gotos and largest conditional bodies 369/379 nodes (limit 405), but the image estimate has unmeasured fixed runtime overhead. **Recommendation:** disposition as an explicitly unverified console-compilation risk; freeze these bytes and require a real PPLC/Forth compile before acquisition. Any source change needs a new size check. No speculative further feature removal is justified by this model alone.

2. **LOW — the RF phase correction depends on delivered hardware timing.** v1.82 lines 2635–2645, 3807–3858 and 3968–4060, and v1.912 lines 3214–3231 and 4118–4159, implement fixed offset windows and persistent phase arithmetic. The v1.912 header correctly states the nominal 3-us RF latency and manual-cost spans. Manual pp52–54 confirm that a sub-MHz offset accumulates phase, `reset_frequency()` delivers frequency after 23.7 us, and nonstandard phase increments deliver phase after 28.3 us; restoring a frequency buffer does not clear that history. Independent integration of delivered commands gives RF-centre relative phase errors below 0.21 degrees at ±5 mm. This establishes internal consistency of the nominal source model, not the actual oscillator, receiver phase, or timing of the console installation. **Recommendation:** retain the existing phantom odd/even-phase pass criterion and inspect complex navigator phase across shots and slices before in-vivo use. The review does not claim an RF/receiver system measurement.

3. **INFORMATION — position units and the RF transition margin need the planned console/phantom confirmation.** v1.912 lines 2379–2397 and 3185–3196 use 400 offset units/mm, actual selection DACs and the 0.75-thickness transition allowance. That position convention is consistent with the repository generator/recon convention and the 1-mm acquired controls. The inherited frequency calculation also contains a legacy bandwidth/thickness assumption, so its old arithmetic alone cannot prove the console convention for every thickness. The shipped-pulse margin report uses a 5% RF-response criterion; it does not guarantee whole-sequence signal isolation. **Recommendation:** retain the stated scope and the minimum-spacing crosstalk and slice-position scanner checks. This is a validation limitation, not an established placement defect.

## Independent checks and source audit

Read the user handoff, both transformation builders, the changed PPL regions, prior compiler/PPL findings and final reviews, FINAL_REPORT timing/moment/b-tensor sections, the new report/test plan, and local vendor-manual pp52–56. The review focused on the previously reported timer, signed-width and live-matrix hazards rather than introducing a new simulation framework.

- **Exact reproduction:** called `generate_ppl()`/`generate_ppr()` for v1.82 and the non-writing `build()` for v1.912; all bytes matched the frozen files. The builders verify immutable-source hashes and use anchored transformations. The diffusion DAC selection and diffusion-180 crusher construction are outside their changed regions.
- **Static compiler audit rerun:** both candidates pass against their actual immutable baselines. Evidence: `independent_static.json`. No undefined labels, added long literals, risky slash-comment macro calls, new function-like macros or new W007/W008 signatures were found.
- **Timing, matrix and navigator probe:** separately mapped both versions at 25-us dwell and minimum ESP (11/10 ms), mode 2, three slices at −2.8/0/+2.8 mm, with dummies disabled and six shots each, in all eight cost models. This is 96 mapped shots. Every shot emitted nine ADCs. All runs had zero timer overruns, ignored lists and premature matrix uses on S/P/R. Appended navigator PE moments passed the 0.03-cycles/m criterion in every run. Largest ADC/RF-midpoint error was 15.30 us for v1.82 and 9.25 us for v1.912, below the established 25-us tolerance. Evidence: `independent_review_checks.py`, `independent_events.json`.
- **Phase probe independent of the new phase helper:** integrated piecewise delivered frequency commands without treating `reset_frequency()` or `resync()` as a phase reset. Checked centre and ±5-mm positions for every RF in one nominal shot, relative to a common shot phase origin. Maximum relative RF-centre error was 0.207 degrees (v1.82) and 0.197 degrees (v1.912). v1.912 preparation, tip-up, re-excitation and train RFs use their own actual selection-gradient offsets. Evidence: `independent_phase_checks.py`, `independent_phase.json`.
- **Fused S areas:** compared every RF/ADC boundary interval with v1.911 at 50-us dwell/13-ms ESP, then with v1.912 at that control and at 25-us dwell/10 and 11 ms. Maximum S-interval difference as a fraction of reference |D| was **0** in these nominal probes. The 1328-us fused tops, E142 residual/lattice checks, C/D area arithmetic and 200/700/1000/60-us geometry remain intact. Evidence: `independent_fused.json`.
- **Below-minimum rejection:** own nominal maps at v1.82 50/25-us dwell and ESP 13/10, and v1.912 ESP 12/9, emit no ADC and print the correct minimum 14/11 and 13/10 ms respectively. The v1.912 failures report E147. These guards are in setup before the RF kernel. Evidence: `independent_minimum.json`.
- **Navigator source audit:** all `nav_on` branches in the final files were inspected. Modes 1 and 2 retain the leading per-volume train; mode 2 normalizes the raw row count before PE-table/loop setup and adds one physical echo without advancing the imaging view counter by the extra echo. The appended PE coefficient is zero; v1.912 reuses the last imaging flip and fused train geometry. The sampled negative prephaser is mirrored with an inverted secondary P coefficient in mode 2, while modes 0/1 retain the prior positive waveform. The coefficient change occurs in the zero-P interval, and the independent ledger finds no nonzero matrix use before DSP readiness. The terminal v1.912 matrix remains behind the inherited 24000-tick anchor, with 28000/40000-tick playback/exit anchors; the old terminal live-P/R rewrite is not reintroduced.
- **Integer/timer audit:** the new phase arithmetic uses long products, modulo 10,000,000 tick-Hz, then bounded conversion to 0.225-degree units. v1.82 explicitly guards its RF-span product against signed-32 overflow. v1.912 restricts its per-width offsets to ±32767 Hz and uses the fixed shipped RF lengths; those span products are within signed-32 range. The existing fused-area overflow guard remains. The accepted timer range remains a physical representation constraint, not a simulated ESP list. The inherited signed-int representation of 40000-tick waits is unchanged and still depends on the controller's unsigned timer-word interpretation; this is documented precedent, not newly verified hardware behavior.

## Limitations and disposition

The independent runs supplement, rather than rerun, the author's complete feature validator. Existing mode-0/1 preservation results, reconstruction/archived-MRD tests, ADC effective-b fits and RF-profile calculations were read as author evidence; this pass did not independently repeat all of them. The review's independent experiments cover the final PPL bytes and selected numeric/timing/physics boundaries. They do not prove the entire accepted parameter space.

No vendor cpp/PPLC/Forth toolchain, scanner event trace, hardware calibration, finite-RF off-centre Bloch grid, acquired mode-2 dataset or physical slice-crosstalk measurement was available or claimed. A common shot phase is physically arbitrary for the RF-relation check; removing it in that check must not be described as measuring absolute transmit/receive phase or shot-to-shot acquired image phase. The documented source-model checks and phantom scanner plan are the appropriate next step. The low-risk items above require explicit parent disposition; they do not require another independent review cycle.
