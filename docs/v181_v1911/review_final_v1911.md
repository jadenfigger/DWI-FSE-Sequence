## Findings (severity ranked)

### 1. HIGH (coordinator-confirmed; not re-derived) - terminal CREATE_MATRIX rewrites matrix 259 while P/R trailing lobes play
Ledger on v1911: `matrix 259 used 235.6 us before DSP ready on P` and `... on R`, once per shot (v7 ledger: zero P/R issues). Shot 1: last ADC complete 188422.2 us; trailing sec P/R lobes (2752 / 269 DAC, 700-us flat) are playing 188187.9-189279.9 us; terminal matrix call lands inside them. Coefficients are unchanged (same gp_var, gr_dp), so ideal output is unchanged, but hardware behaviour during a DSP recompute is unknown. S is clean: S lobe starts after the matrix is ready. Raw `matrix_active` flags also rise by exactly one per shot (51 vs 48 over 3 shots, 136 vs 128 over 8). In-loop creates never coincide with sec P/R lobes (pre-lobes end ~263 us before initiate); only the terminal one does. Mitigation idea (unverified): create the terminal S coefficient in a matrix not carrying live P/R lobes, or defer it until the P/R lobes finish.

### 2. MEDIUM - terminal timers are unguarded against filter length (tfilter) and statement cost
Added path after `starttimer()` (post adc_mid): `delay32(10000)` + `complete()` (=tfilter) + freq/phase calls + CREATE_MATRIX (135.6) + `delay(caldelay)` + SetList must finish inside `waittimer(18000)`. Measured use: 16725-16905 of 18000 ticks across the 8 cost models, i.e. 110-125 us margin at tfilter = 373 us. With `acqpad_ticks` swept: 4600 OK (S lobe 343 us after complete); 5000 -> `waittimer(18000)` overrun, +5 ms, and `waittimer(30000)` also extended (+5 ms); the terminal S lobe then starts 5303 us after complete. Threshold tfilter ~ 496 us (+33%). The existing guard (post_b >= 10000 + tfilter*10 + 1000) covers the loop, not this path (needs ~+240 us extra). No guard was added. Manual quoted 380 us for SW < 100 kHz, so likely fine at the validated protocol, but sample_period/bandwidth is user-selectable (SPECTRAL_WIDTH 1000..200000). Recommend a guard or a wider 18000 anchor. Consequence of overrun is a 5-ms later lobe/longer shot (tr_min carries +5000 us slack), not a wrong moment.

### 3. LOW - TR floor not exact at high statement cost (inherited)
With TR = ceil(tr_min): v1911 periods minus TR (shots 2-3) = -154..-17 us for all models except `flat_2us` where +129 us (v7: +137). tr_min 191 ms vs 198 ms (v7); TR = tr_min-ms is rejected ("increase to 192 ms" vs 199 ms) in all models; zero timer overruns. The shot_us formula (`+(templ1+30000)/10 - (read_len+10+TAIL)`) is consistent with the new tail. Not a regression (v7 shows the same).

### 5. LOW - cost-model timing artifact in R interval comparison
