# Independent PPL/compiler review

Reviewer: separate from the v1.81 and v1.911 sequence authors. Date: 2026-10-08.
Review is in progress; no final candidate approval is recorded yet. The reviewer
does not modify sequence builders or sequence/protocol sources.

## Findings returned to authors

1. The initial v1.81 source (`3978fb01...541c24`) had 151 forward gotos, exceeding
   the empirical E106 threshold, and estimated 57454 program-model bytes versus
   56664 for v1.8. The author was advised to use the established standalone
   backward exit hub, rather than enter a label inside an if body. Current
   builder revisions adopt the known hub; final regenerated bytes remain to
   be checked. Image growth needs compensation or an explicitly revised and
   justified acceptance decision; compiler success cannot be inferred from
   the model.
2. The initial v1.911 builder left the PPR pointing to v1.91. This was corrected
   before candidate `c3a7aa8e...da0d54`.
3. An initial v1.911 matrix insertion began another calculation before the
   preceding calculation's settling delay. The revised builder replaces the
   two old imaging matrices with the two needed acquisition matrices and
   preserves the intervening `caldelay`. Final ledger review is separate.
4. The v1.911 area scaling initially multiplied arbitrary accepted areas by 100
   without an explicit signed-32 bound. The author added a precheck of
   `-(C_area+D_area)<=21474000`. Including denominator/2, the largest rounded
   numerator is 2147476496, below 2147483647. The per-result residual products
   are correspondingly bounded. No unsafe accepted protocol was established
   from the initial overflow concern; the concern is avoiding reliance on
   wrapped arithmetic rather than claiming demonstrated scanner behavior.
5. The reviewed intermediate area calculation uses `scale(DAC,19200,10000)`.
   Its result is a signed int and can overflow before the later bound rejects
   large scaled inputs. The author was advised to use a long-only expression
   `(0L+DAC)*19200L/10000L`, preserving nominal truncation without narrowing.
   This is pending final source inspection.
