# Scanner experiments — October 12, 2026

All 24 acquisitions (22 FSE and 2 lab EPI) were verified using their MRD-embedded PPR, adjacent PPR, reconstructed NIfTI header where one exists, and JSON and diffusion sidecars where they exist. Each FSE embedded protocol was diffed against the planned PPR it was run from ([2026-10-12 plan](2026-10-12_exvivo_invivo_plan/README.md); [run order](2026-10-12_exvivo_invivo_plan/run_order.csv)); the in-vivo scans were not planned as run and are compared with the closest plan letter. Scans are listed in plan order with the unplanned MM, Q1 and QQQ after their references, then EPI test0, the in-vivo scans iA–iF (folders `invivoA`…`invivoF`) and EPI invivoA; the chronology table gives actual acquisition-counter order. **Planned scans H, I, J, K, L, P, S, T, U, V, W, X and the navigator-1 fallbacks RR, SS, TT, UU, VV, WW, XX were not acquired.** QQ is the only fallback acquired. Unplanned acquisitions: MM, Q1, QQQ, EPI-test0, iA, iB, iC, iD, iE, iF, EPI-invivoA. The `v183_testO` folder is empty; the acquired O is in `v1912_testO`. N has an MRD, PPR and protocol_notes.json but no reconstruction. Embedded metadata takes precedence where PPRs differ. The folder PPRs of iA (`v183_testA`), iD (`v183_testD`), iE (`v183_testE`), iF (`v183_testF`) carry plan-letter names but their content matches the embedded in-vivo protocol, not those plans; the adjacent PPR matches the embedded protocol for 21 of 24 scans (differences: N, R, EPI-test0). A version label identifies the recorded scanner PPL path; it does not prove the source or build used on the scanner.

**Sample provenance:** A–R and EPI test0 are a fixed rat brain in a PBS-filled tube with the volume coil; iA–iF and EPI invivoA are in-vivo rat brain with the surface coil (as stated for the session; fixation history, animal details, anaesthesia, temperature and shim were not supplied). The MRD does not record the centre frequency (`:OBSERVE_FREQUENCY` holds a 0.0 placeholder), and the outcome of the A/B frequency check is not part of this audit.

All FSE scans use centric phase encoding (`PE_order=1`), one average, 35-mm FOV, 128 samples and 128 imaging lines, b-input mode 1, δ 4 ms, zero in-plane FOV offsets, and the same crusher settings (first-echo / train amplitude −5482 / −8223 DAC, constant schedule). Otherwise TE 44 ms except iE, iF (54 ms); ESP 11 ms except A (14 ms); TR 2000 ms except iD, iE, iF (3000 ms); Δ 30 ms except iE, iF (40 ms); dwell 25 µs except A (50 µs); slice thickness 1 mm except iE, iF (2 mm); nav_on 1 except N, R (2); ETL 8 except iF (16). ETL means echo train length; navigator views are additional raw lines, not extra imaging lines. For `nav_on=2` the stored `VIEWS_PER_SEGMENT` is 9 (8 echoes plus the per-shot navigator echo) and the 153 raw views are one navigator segment plus 16 shots of 8 imaging lines each followed by one navigator echo. Directions are in the scanner logical axes as stored (x = read, y = phase, z = slice). The planned PPRs carry no gain records, so gains come from the MRDs only.
